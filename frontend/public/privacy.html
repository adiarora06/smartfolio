SmartFolio Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

SmartFolio provides educational analysis only. It is not financial advice, it does not execute trades, and it is not a broker or adviser.

The short version

SmartFolio has no accounts and asks for no personal identity. There is no name, email, phone number, address, or password anywhere in the app. No advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites. The app works fully offline.

What is stored, and where

DataWhere it livesWhy
Portfolio holdings, investor preferences (age band, horizon, risk tolerance), saved research memos On your device, and — if the app reaches our analysis service — in our database, keyed only to a random workspace identifier To calculate your allocation, risk profile, and analysis, and to keep them across launches
A random workspace identifier (e.g. 6f10475f…) Your device's local storage The only thing linking your saved data to your app. It is generated on device, contains nothing about you, and is never linked to an identity
Tickers you analyze Sent to our analysis service; cached briefly To fetch prices and produce a forecast

What is sent to third parties

ServiceWhat it receives
Market data providers (Finnhub, Alpha Vantage, Yahoo) Only the ticker symbol being analyzed. No portfolio data, no identifier.
AI provider (OpenAI, or Anthropic as a fallback) The numbers a memo or advisor answer describes — which can include your holdings, weights, and analysis figures — so the text can explain them. Sent without any identifier. Used only to generate that response. If no AI provider is configured, the app writes the text itself and nothing leaves our service.
Plaid (only if you choose to connect a brokerage) Read-only access to holdings, used once to import them. We never store the access token, and SmartFolio cannot move money or place trades.
Hosting and error reporting (Render, Neon, Sentry) Standard request logs and, if a crash occurs, diagnostic details. Not used to profile you.

Financial information

Holdings and values are financial information, and we disclose them as such on the App Store. They are used solely to compute and explain your analysis. They are never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to build a profile of you.

Deleting your data

Because there is no account, your data is reachable only through the random workspace identifier on your device. Deleting the app removes that identifier, which permanently disconnects the stored data. To have the server-side record erased outright, send the identifier (shown in the app under More → status) to the contact address below.

Children

SmartFolio is not directed to children under 13.
